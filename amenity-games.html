The Space Let off steam

Loud, fast and
unserious

The games room is the house's loudest corner — a regulation table tennis table, rackets and balls ready, and vintage boxing prints watching over the play. It is built for energy, not etiquette.

Drop in after the gym, bring a friend, play until someone concedes. The room is always on, and no one keeps a formal score.

The Ritual First to eleven

Losers clear
the table

The games room runs on house rules: first to eleven wins, the loser clears the table, and the next two step up. It is fast, loud, and deliberately unserious — a release valve after a day of meetings or a long dinner.

No booking, no equipment to find, just walk in and play.

The only room in the house where being loud is the whole point.— The Games Room
