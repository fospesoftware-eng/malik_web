At The Residence on East Parade, entertaining is designed to feel personal, effortless and refined.

Our private dining room offers residents an elegant setting for intimate dinners, special occasions and memorable gatherings with family and friends. Supported by a state-of-the-art show kitchen and Teppanyaki bar, the space creates a unique dining experience rarely found within residential living.

Whether celebrating a birthday, marking a special occasion or simply bringing loved ones together, the Private Dining & Chef's Table provides an exceptional setting for shared experiences and unforgettable hospitality.
