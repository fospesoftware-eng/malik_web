03 — Private Dining

A table reserved
only for you

Michelin-trained chefs, a master sommelier and a single seating each evening — hosted in your residence, or at the chef’s table overlooking the sea.

Four experiences The Malik Table

Dining, written
around your evening

There is no restaurant, and no second seating. Every dinner is composed, cooked and served solely for your party.

Breakfast through late supper
