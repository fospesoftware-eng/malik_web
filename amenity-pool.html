The Space Swim against the current

A pool that never
ends

The counter-current system lets you swim in place for as long as you like — no turns, no crowding, just water moving past you at the pace you set. Set it gentle for a recovery swim, or push it hard for a proper session.

Around it, a walled brick courtyard keeps the city at bay. The louvered pergola softens the midday sun; the timber deck stays warm underfoot long after it sets. Towels, cold drinks and a monsoon shower are kept within reach.

The Ritual From dawn to dusk

Swim at dawn,
stretch at noon

The pool is quietest at first light — the courtyard still in shadow, the water glass-still before the current wakes it. By noon the pergola throws slatted light across the deck, and the loungers become the day's second office.

In the evening the brick walls hold the day's warmth, and the pool turns gold. It is the house's most consistent pleasure, available whenever you are.

The only courtyard in the house where the clock runs slower.— The Endless Pool
