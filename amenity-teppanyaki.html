The Space Dinner at the griddle

A chef, a griddle,
your table

The teppanyaki bar wraps around a single iron griddle, with seating for ten on high stools that put you close to the action. A chef cooks your dinner in front of you — slicing, searing, seasoning — while the room fills with the smell of hot butter and garlic.

Choose a set menu or bring your own brief. The bar is yours for the evening; the chef works to your pace.

The Ritual Heat and theatre

The show is the
dinner

The griddle heats first, then the butter, then the chef's knives. Dinner unfolds course by course in front of you — a performance as much as a meal, with sake poured between acts and the room warm with conversation.

It is the house's most social dinner — loud, warm, and impossible to rush. Book it for a group; the room is better full.

The only room in the house where the chef is the entertainment.— The Teppanyaki Bar
