The communals of The Residence — wellness, work, play and the long table — photographed as they are, ready whenever you are.

01

The Endless Pool & Sun Deck

A counter-current swim pool set in a walled brick courtyard, shaded by a louvered pergola and wrapped in warm timber decking. Swim against the current at dawn, stretch out under slatted light by noon — towels, showers and cold drinks kept within reach.

  • Counter-current "endless" swim pool
  • Louvered pergola & timber sun deck
  • Walled brick garden courtyard
Explore Pool & Sun Deck
02

The Performance Gym

A blackout-lit training floor built for proper sessions — twin Taurus rigs, a sprint turf lane and a dumbbell ladder that climbs past fifty kilos. Train solo or bring in a coach; the room resets itself after you.

  • Twin Taurus rigs & sprint turf
  • Full dumbbell & bench line-up
  • Personal training on request
Explore Performance Gym
03

Spa, Sauna & Steam

The quiet floor. A cedar-lined sauna washed in amber light, a marble steam room and a monsoon shower to finish — with black-and-gold marble guest bathrooms kept ready beside them. No bookings, no crowds; just heat, water and stillness.

  • Cedar sauna & marble steam room
  • Monsoon shower & rest areas
  • Marble guest bathrooms adjoining
Explore Spa, Sauna & Steam
04

The Cinema

Red velvet recliners, a fibre-optic star ceiling and a wall-sized screen — the cinema runs first runs and old favourites alike. Settle in with popcorn and a blanket; the projectionist handles the rest.

  • Velvet recliner seating
  • Fibre-optic star ceiling
  • Wall-sized screen & surround sound
Explore The Cinema
05

The Teppanyaki Bar

Black marble counters traced in gold, cream leather stools and front-row seats to the cooking. Book the chef's counter for an evening of teppanyaki theatre — or let the room become your private dining story for the night.

  • Black & gold marble grill counters
  • Chef's table counter seating
  • Private chef bookings
Explore Teppanyaki Bar
06

Dining Hall & Study

Under a row of skylights, one long table seats the whole house for breakfast, slow dinners and everything in between. Beyond it, a quiet study corner with desk space and shelf-lit walls for the working hours.

  • Skylit communal dining hall
  • Long-table private dining
  • Quiet study & reading corner
