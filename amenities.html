02 — Amenities & Lifestyle

Everyday, elevated
to art

A private world of wellness, taste and leisure across the estate — attended quietly, around the clock, and included with every residence.

01

Infinity Pool & Beach Club

A 25-metre horizon pool appears to merge with the bay, flanked by private cabanas and a sunset terrace. The Beach Club below offers a powder-sand cove with paddleboards, snorkelling and chilled service from dawn to last light.

  • 25-metre heated horizon pool
  • Private cabanas & beach butlers
  • Water sports & sunset bar
02

Private Spa & Hammam

Sculpted in cream marble, the spa houses five treatment suites, a thermal hammam, vitality pool and relaxation gallery. Resident therapists practice signature rituals using marine minerals and cold-pressed botanicals.

  • Five treatment suites & couples room
  • Thermal hammam & vitality pool
  • In-residence treatments on request
03

Cinema & Cigar Lounge

A Dolby Atmos private cinema in velvet and oak, with reclining loungers for eighteen and a curator who secures pre-release screenings. A humidor lounge with a rare-spirits bar sits just beyond the doors.

  • Dolby Atmos & 4K laser projection
  • Curated film library & premieres
  • Humidor & rare-spirits lounge
04

Fitness & Yoga Pavilion

A light-filled studio with the latest Technogym line faces the tropical gardens, joined by an open-air yoga pavilion and recovery suite with ice bath, infrared sauna and compression therapy.

  • Resident PTs & private classes
  • Open-air yoga & meditation pavilion
  • Ice bath, sauna & recovery suite
05

Wine Cellar & Tasting Room

A 3,000-bottle cellar travels from Burgundy to the Bekaa Valley, with a marble tasting room and a master sommelier who hosts private flights, vintner dinners and pairings for every residence.

  • 3,000-bottle climate-controlled cellar
  • Private tastings & vintner dinners
  • Sommelier-curated in-villa bars
06

Concierge & Valet

Round-the-clock concierge, valet and a fleet of chauffeured vehicles handle everything from restaurant rows to private aviation. One message, before you even know you need it.

  • 24-hour concierge desk & in-app
  • Chauffeurs, valet & yacht tenders
  • Private aviation & helicopter coordination
