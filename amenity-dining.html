The Space The long table

The house's long
table

A single long table runs the length of the dining hall — big enough for sixteen, intimate enough for four. Beside it, a quiet study lined with books and a writing desk for the hours when the table is too loud.

Cater privately, bring your own food, or use it as an extended office for the day. The hall is the house's most flexible room — it becomes whatever the moment needs.

The Ritual Gather, then settle

A long table,
a quiet study

The dining hall is loudest at dinner — candles down the table, everyone leaning in. The study is quietest in the morning: a single desk, good light, and the house still asleep around you.

Between the two, the house has a room for every pace of the day. Use them together or separately; they are always ready.

The only room in the house that works as well for a board meeting as for a birthday.— The Dining Hall & Study
