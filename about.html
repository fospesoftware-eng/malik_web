Malik Living A New Approach

A New Approach
To City Living

Malik Living is a premium residential operator dedicated to delivering an entirely new standard of city living — one that combines beautiful design, genuine wellbeing, seamless service and meaningful community.

We believe that exceptional living is defined not only by architecture and interiors, but by the quality of everyday experience. Every Malik Living residence has been carefully created around this belief.

The Residence on East Parade is our flagship collection — 130 private residences within three restored Georgian terrace buildings in the heart of Leeds city centre.

Our Philosophy What We Believe In

Six principles
that shape how we live

Every Malik Living residence is built around these core beliefs — from design to technology to sustainability.

Design-Led Living

Every residence and shared space is designed with care, intention and beauty. We believe the places we inhabit shape how we feel and who we become.

Wellbeing First

Wellness is woven into the everyday at Malik Living. From private spa facilities to rooftop spaces, every amenity supports a healthier, more balanced life.

Community & Connection

We create environments that bring people together. A curated calendar of resident experiences fosters genuine connection and a true sense of belonging.

Seamless Service

From the resident app to the concierge team, every touchpoint has been designed to feel effortless, intuitive and genuinely helpful from day one.

Technology Integrated

Smart building systems, high-speed connectivity and a dedicated resident app ensure life at The Residence flows without friction or complication.

Sustainable Future

We are committed to a more responsible approach to urban living — through solar energy, EV charging, efficient systems and thoughtful long-term design.
