The Space A private screening room

Your own cinema,
on call

Acoustically lined, fully dark, with a screen that fills one wall and surround sound that fills the rest. Reclining seats run in two rows, with side tables for drinks and a concessions bar just outside the door.

Book it for a film, a match, or a presentation — the room handles whatever you put on screen. Popcorn, drinks and blankets are a message away.

Lights down,
sound up

The cinema is at its best after dinner — the house quiet, the seats warm, the screen the only light. Send a message to the concierge and the room is ready: film queued, drinks poured, popcorn in bowls.

It works just as well for the midday match or a work presentation. The room adapts; the comfort never drops.
