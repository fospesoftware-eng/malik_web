06 The House of Malik

A quarter-century of
quiet obsession

Malik began as a single family studio with a single conviction — that a home should feel inevitable to its place. Twenty-five years later, the studio has delivered one hundred and twenty residences across fourteen cities, yet each one begins the same way: with the land, the light, and the life that will unfold within it.

No two Malik homes are alike. Each is conceived by hand, finished by master craftsmen, and attended for life by a service team that anticipates before it is asked.
