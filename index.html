05 The House of Malik

A house finished to
the last detail

The Residence began with a single conviction — that a city stay should feel like a private club without ever losing the ease of home. Every surface in the four residences was considered: the gold trim in the showers, the weight of the hotel linen, the quiet line of the bespoke joinery.

Beyond the front door the house widens — pool, gym, spa, cinema, teppanyaki bar and dining hall — all of it attended daily by a team that anticipates before it is asked.
